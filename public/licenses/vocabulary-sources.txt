Section 4 companion
60 topic word selections are independently curated for Kotoba, not copied
lesson word lists. They follow the factual unit goals in this community
Japanese-from-English course outline, dated March 5, 2026:
https://duolingodata.com/dat/jafen1030.html#section4
The selections are reviewed with JMdict and adapted to their topic context.
These companion lists are not a verified export of words introduced by unit.
Duolingo course versions, unit placement, and vocabulary may differ.

Memory cue dictionary data
KANJIDIC2, copyright the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group.
https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/KANJIDIC_Project.html
https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
CC BY-SA 4.0. Up to two English meanings per used character are selected as
memory cues, not as an etymological explanation or a Chinese translation.
JMdict supplies word classes, matched to the bundled meaning and reading.
JSON conversion: scriptin/jmdict-simplified, release 3.6.2+20260928191014.
Selected data: src/data/memory-lexicon.json. Extraction script:
scripts/extract-memory-lexicon.py. Original Chinese cues, short Japanese practice
phrases, and recall prompts are maintained separately in the app's memory data.
No Tatoeba example sentences are redistributed by the memory lexicon extractor.
